About the Coach

Emanuel Vaglio

I’m a FIDE Candidate Master (CM) from San José, Costa Rica, known on Lichess as Olaffo. Chess has been my lifelong passion — from learning the moves as a child to earning a FIDE rating of 2296 and competing in national and international tournaments.

With over 5 years of dedicated coaching experience, I specialize in helping club players rated 1000–1600 find the patterns, habits, and thought processes that lead to real, lasting improvement. My lessons are bilingual — English and Spanish — so you can learn in whichever language feels most natural.

My coaching philosophy centers on understanding over memorization. I want you to develop genuine chess intuition, not just opening theory. Whether you struggle with tactics, time management, or positional play, every session is tailored to your specific needs and goals.
